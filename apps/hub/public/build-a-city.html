Build a city

Your architecture. Your community. Your destinations.

WorldMesh is one possible city for discovering independent 3D worlds, not a template everyone must copy. Imagine a forest of portals, a game arcade, a gallery, a virtual downtown or a social plaza, each linking visitors to worlds hosted anywhere.

What city operators could control

  • Architecture: the layout, design, movement and atmosphere of their own city.
  • Discovery: which worlds to feature, recommendations, rankings and collections.
  • Community: social experiences, events and moderation policies.
  • Business: their own sponsorships, advertising or paid placements, disclosed to visitors and subject to applicable rules.
  • Infrastructure: their own hosting, traffic and operational costs.

What you can do now

The WorldMesh repository is open source. You can study its hub and reuse its MIT-licensed runtime to develop a separate 3D experience, and link to independent destinations. You can connect a Three.js world with shared controls and optional presence.

Current limitation: the repository does not yet offer a turnkey multi-city deployment or a verified shared sign-in system for independently operated cities.

What comes next

  1. Document a minimal city discovery and interoperability contract.
  2. Test an independent second city on a different domain.
  3. Design portable user identity and permissions across operators.
  4. Validate avatar continuity and city-to-city handoff, including safe fallbacks.
  5. Publish a straightforward guide for operators to run and monetize their own cities.

There is no franchise application or license fee to join today because the independent city network is still a proposal. The intention is an open protocol, not a city chain controlled by one company.

Follow development

Explore the protocol overview and GitHub repository. The present priority is helping people discover worlds; a larger city ecosystem will require demonstrated interoperability first.
