Connect your world

Already made a 3D world? Copy one prompt into the AI coding agent you already use, and it connects your world to WorldMesh for you. Your world stays yours, on your own hosting.

  • MultiplayerVisitors see each other, through your own multiplayer or a free relay you own.
  • Portable avatarsVisitors arrive as the character they picked on WorldMesh.
  • One identityThe same character in every connected world. No sign-up in yours.
  • PortalsWalk from your world into others, and straight in from WorldMesh where supported.

1. Copy this prompt

Connect this 3D world to WorldMesh (https://worldmesh.net). Work in this project as it is on my computer. You do not need GitHub access, a pull request or a WorldMesh account. 1. Read the official guide at https://worldmesh.net/connect.md and follow it step by step. If you cannot open web pages, tell me and I will paste the guide in. 2. Inspect the project first: engine, render loop, controls, player, physics, any existing multiplayer, and how it is deployed. 3. Keep my existing gameplay, controls, rendering and multiplayer working. Ask me before removing or replacing any of them. 4. Do not use the multiplayer relay WorldMesh hosts. Use my existing multiplayer, or set up a WorldMesh relay on my own hosting as the guide explains. 5. Ask me before creating accounts, adding paid services, deploying, or committing and pushing. 6. When you are done, tell me plainly what works, what you tested, and what is still left for me to do. Do not say it is finished if it is not.

2. Paste it into your AI coding agent

Claude, Cursor, Replit, Codex, Copilot or similar. Open your world's project in it and paste. The agent works on your own copy, so private projects are fine. No GitHub access, pull request or WorldMesh account is needed.

If your agent cannot open web pages, use Copy full guide and paste that too.

When your agent will ask you

  • Before it replaces your controls, player, physics or multiplayer. It keeps them unless you agree.
  • Before it creates or uses an account. Multiplayer without your own server needs a free Cloudflare account you sign in to.
  • Before it deploys anything or commits your code.

At the end it tells you what works, what it tested and what is left for you. If something could not be done safely, it explains the remaining steps instead.

3. Publish

  1. Deploy your world wherever you host it.
  2. Submit its URL with Submit world on the WorldMesh homepage. Submissions are reviewed before they appear.

Interested in an independent 3D discovery city instead? Read Build a city and the protocol overview. Multi-city sign-in and federation are not available yet.

Which worlds work

  • Three.js and React Three Fiber: everything above.
  • Other engines (Babylon.js, PlayCanvas, A-Frame, Unity, Godot and others): your world can still be listed if it uses the usual controls (WASD, mouse, Space), but it cannot get WorldMesh multiplayer, avatars or portals yet.

Who pays for multiplayer

WorldMesh does not host multiplayer for your world, so it stays under your control. Your agent keeps the multiplayer you already have, or sets up a WorldMesh relay on your own Cloudflare account. It runs on Cloudflare's free plan, and you can raise its 16-person room limit.

Prefer to do it yourself?

npm i @worldmesh/runtime three
import { createWorldMesh } from '@worldmesh/runtime';

createWorldMesh({ scene, camera, renderer, avatar: { source: 'worldmesh' } });

Pass the scene, camera and renderer you already have, add colliders for what visitors stand on, and remove your own camera controls. The integration guide covers multiplayer and the rest, and the reference lists every option.
