Connect your world

Give your Three.js world the shared WorldMesh controls, portals to other worlds, and multiplayer, so visitors see each other. Your world stays on your own hosting. No account, no server.

Hand this to your AI coding agent

Connect this 3D world to WorldMesh. Read https://worldmesh.net/connect.md and follow it step by step.

Works with Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Copilot and similar agents. If your agent cannot open web pages, use Copy full instructions and paste everything instead.

Or do it yourself

npm i @worldmesh/runtime three
import { loadWorldMesh } from '@worldmesh/runtime';

const world = await loadWorldMesh({ scene, camera, renderer, multiplayer: true });

Pass the scene, camera and renderer you already have. loadWorldMesh runs the current WorldMesh runtime, so your world gets fixes and improvements without you updating or redeploying anything. Add colliders for the meshes visitors should stand on, and remove your own camera controls. The full instructions cover the rest.

Then

  1. Deploy your world wherever you host it.
  2. Submit its URL with Submit world on the WorldMesh homepage. Submissions are reviewed before they appear.

Multiplayer: your choice

WorldMesh gives your world its controls, portals and a place on the hub. Which server lets your visitors see each other is up to you, and you can switch any time:

  1. WorldMesh's free relay. Pass multiplayer: true. Free within limits: up to 16 people in a world at once, and 240 active minutes per world each day (UTC; only time when someone is moving counts). After that, new visitors are single-player until midnight UTC.
  2. Your own relay. The same relay on your Cloudflare account: deploy it in one click, then pass multiplayer: { server: 'wss://…' }. Everything works the same, with no daily limit; the traffic is on your account, and the Workers free plan covers most worlds. Settings and manual steps.
  3. Your own backend. Already on Nakama, Colyseus, PartyKit, Firebase, Supabase, AWS or a server of your own? Pass a small network adapter instead, and nothing goes through WorldMesh. A few things the relay does for you, such as chat bubbles and “World is full”, are then yours to build. The guide has a complete example.
