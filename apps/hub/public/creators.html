Connect your world

Give your Three.js world the shared WorldMesh controls, portals to other worlds, and multiplayer, so visitors see each other. Your world stays on your own hosting. No account, no server.

Hand this to your AI coding agent

Connect this 3D world to WorldMesh. Read https://worldmesh.net/connect.md and follow it step by step.

Works with Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Copilot and similar agents. If your agent cannot open web pages, use Copy full instructions and paste everything instead.

Or do it yourself

npm i @worldmesh/runtime three
import { createWorldMesh } from '@worldmesh/runtime';

createWorldMesh({ scene, camera, renderer, multiplayer: true });

Pass the scene, camera and renderer you already have. Add colliders for the meshes visitors should stand on, and remove your own camera controls. The full instructions cover the rest.

Then

  1. Deploy your world wherever you host it.
  2. Submit its URL with Submit world on the WorldMesh homepage. Submissions are reviewed before they appear.

Good to know

  • Only Three.js worlds can use the runtime. Worlds made with other engines can still be listed if they use the standard controls.
  • Up to 16 people can be in a world at once. Need more? Run your own relay and pass multiplayer: { server: 'wss://…' }.
  • See the terms and what multiplayer shares in the privacy policy.
