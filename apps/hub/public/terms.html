The service

WorldMesh is a free, open directory of independently hosted 3D worlds. We provide a hub that links to worlds — we do not host, proxy, or mirror the worlds themselves.

Submitting a world

  • You must own or have permission to submit the world URL.
  • Submissions are reviewed before appearing on the hub. We may reject or remove any world at our discretion.
  • By submitting, you grant WorldMesh permission to display your world's name, description, creator name, portfolio link, and a preview screenshot on the hub.

World guidelines

WorldMesh is open and creation-agnostic — you can build your world with any engine, framework, AI model, tool, hosting provider, or workflow you like. The only compatibility requirement is navigation:

  • Worlds should use familiar PC game controls for basic navigation (e.g. WASD or arrow keys for movement, mouse for looking around, Space for jumping, Shift for sprinting, and familiar interaction keys like E).
  • This ensures users can move through any world without relearning controls each time.
  • Mobile support is optional but recommended — touch controls for movement and looking around, tap for interaction, and a portrait-friendly layout.
  • Worlds are free to add additional controls beyond these basics.

By submitting a world, you confirm that it meets this navigation standard.

  • Deceptive or misleading content
