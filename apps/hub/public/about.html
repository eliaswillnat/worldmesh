About WorldMesh

One identity. Infinite worlds. Independent cities.

WorldMesh connects independently hosted 3D worlds across the web. Creators keep their sites, code, assets and hosting. Visitors discover destinations and travel to them through a shared 3D hub.

The first city

WorldMesh's own city focuses on discovery: find worlds, meet other visitors in the hub, and walk through doors to explore. You can also browse the directory without entering the city.

The wider vision

Imagine several independent cities, each with its own architecture, community, destinations and businesses. One might highlight games; another might feature art, virtual events or social spaces. Their operators would choose what to feature and how to support their city.

We want these places to interoperate instead of becoming separate walled gardens. Visitors should eventually be able to use portable identities and characters across participating cities and worlds, and travel without starting over.

A web of worlds, not one centrally owned metaverse.

Open by design

The current WorldMesh compatibility tools include shared Three.js navigation, cross-world links, optional multiplayer presence and an avatar wallet that references models stored with external providers rather than hosting their files on WorldMesh.

Anyone can build and host a world. Our long-term goal is to let anyone run their own city, independently of WorldMesh's own directory or infrastructure. That city-to-city interoperability is a direction under development, not an already available product.

For creators and builders

Connect an existing world today. If you want to operate a discovery city, read Build a city to see what is possible now and what still needs defining.
