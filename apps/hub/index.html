WORLDMESH BETA

A free open network of independently hosted 3D worlds. Walk between them.

Drag to move · Scroll to zoom

WorldMesh worlds should use familiar PC game controls for basic navigation — such as WASD or arrow keys for movement, mouse for looking around, Space for jumping, Shift for sprinting, and familiar interaction keys like E. Mobile support is optional but recommended — touch controls for movement and looking around, tap for interaction, and a portrait-friendly layout. Worlds can add additional controls freely.
